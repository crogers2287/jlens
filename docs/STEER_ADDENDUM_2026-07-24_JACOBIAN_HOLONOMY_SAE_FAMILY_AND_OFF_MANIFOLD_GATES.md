## New external evidence and narrow interpretation

Richards, “Do Active SAE Feature Planes Carry More Holonomy? A Preregistered
Reversal in Gemma,” arXiv `2607.20522`, tests a preregistered semantic-
concentration prediction in Gemma 2 2B. At the final-token layer-12 to layer-13
residual readout, active SAE-feature planes carried less area-normalized
restricted-Jacobian holonomy than matched mixed-feature planes. The adjusted log
contrast was `-0.29439`, with a 95% interval of `[-0.43989, -0.14889]`.

The paper reports that:

1. the active-versus-mixed comparison had common support after matching;
2. the random/mixed/active three-way ordering was undefined because common
   magnitude support failed;
3. the measured geometry was explicitly readout-dependent rather than intrinsic
   to activation space;
4. restricted-Jacobian transport admitted shear and numerical non-orthogonality;
5. matched-center displacement, activation-manifold proximity, dictionary
   geometry, feature engagement, and transport distortion remained live
   mechanisms or confounds; and
6. the result was a narrow operational reversal, not evidence that meaning
   suppresses holonomy.

Cho et al., “Are Single-Token Sparse Autoencoder Features Causally Necessary?
Layer-Depth and SAE-Family Effects,” arXiv `2607.20596`, report full-depth
zero-ablation results over 3.9 million features, six models, and three SAE
families. Cross-family differences exceeded within-family scale effects: features
from some SAE families remained causally anchored while another family showed
local redundancy on the same base model. The target-token rank also commonly
recovered downstream after the same ablation.

These are external author-reported findings, not admitted results of this
repository. No result on either Agents-A1 checkpoint is established.

The defensible correction is:

1. A Jacobian-derived geometric quantity is a property of a fully specified
   measurement instrument, not automatically an intrinsic property of a model.
2. SAE activity does not establish semantic concentration, curvature
   concentration, causal necessity, or monitor value.
3. Plane-selection rules, common support, center construction, loop radius,
   transport distortion, readout identity, backend, and numerical regularization
   can determine a holonomy result.
4. SAE family and training recipe can materially change causal conclusions even
   on the same base model.
5. A preregistered geometric reversal is evidence against silently assuming that
   active or interpretable directions are privileged Jacobian directions.
6. A positive geometric result cannot be promoted to correctness awareness,
   error prediction, planning, or policy utility without independent evidence.

## Required object separation

Every compatible study must bind these objects separately:

1. **Base model artifact:** checkpoint, configuration, tokenizer, quantization,
   runtime, kernels, topology, and serving state.
2. **Activation population:** exact site, layer, token, stage, cache state,
   task population, trajectory boundary, and inclusion rule.
3. **Base point or loop center:** natural activation, matched center, projected
   center, interpolated center, or other constructed point.
4. **Downstream readout map:** the exact map whose Jacobian defines the
   measurement, including held-fixed context, mask, cache, route, and output
   inner product.
5. **Derivative instrument:** JVP, VJP, full Jacobian, finite-difference, or
   approximation method, with admitted numerical parity.
6. **Plane or direction set:** raw directions, SAE decoder directions, PCA
   directions, route-conditioned directions, random directions, or matched
   state differences.
7. **SAE artifact:** family, checkpoint, width, sparsity target, activation
   function, normalization, decoder convention, training corpus, and recipe.
8. **Metric:** Euclidean, pullback, Fisher-like, or another prospectively frozen
   metric, including regularization and degeneracy rules.
9. **Transport rule:** exact segment operator, composition order, pseudoinverse,
   projection, orthogonalization, and closure convention.
10. **Loop construction:** radius, shape, discretization, orientation, direction,
    center, area definition, and radius-selection rule.
11. **Geometric response:** signed rotation, magnitude, area-normalized holonomy,
    shear, stretch, condition number, closure error, or another frozen statistic.
12. **Semantic or mechanistic label:** feature activity, explanation, operation,
    requirement, error class, route function, or other interpretation.
13. **Objective outcome:** independently verified correctness, safety, artifact,
    tool, or environment endpoint.
14. **Intervention:** projection removal, activation addition, feature ablation,
    route forcing, retry, truncation, early exit, or another execution mutation.
15. **Decision policy:** threshold, ranking, escalation, fallback, quarantine, or
    production rule.

Evidence about one object does not admit another. Stable holonomy does not
establish stable semantics. Stable semantics do not establish causal necessity.
Causal influence does not establish objective monitor value. Monitor value does
not establish safe control.

## Instrument-identity gate

Every Jacobian-geometry claim must prospectively freeze:

- model, checkpoint, tokenizer, quantization, runtime, backend, library commits,
  kernels, device, precision, and deterministic settings;
- activation site, layer boundary, token position, prefill/decode stage, cache
  state, attention mask, route state, batch, and serving scheduler;
- downstream map, output site, output inner product, held-fixed context, and any
  patched or recomputed state;
- derivative method, chunking, checkpointing, graph retention, approximation,
  tolerances, and exact parity evidence;
- metric definition, determinant floor, pseudoinverse cutoff, singular-value
  handling, condition-number guard, and degeneracy rejection;
- loop radius, radius calibration, number and placement of points, orientation,
  closure rule, area convention, and response normalization;
- transport operator, multiplication order, orthogonalization or lack thereof,
  and extraction of rotation versus symmetric distortion.

A result may be called intrinsic only after invariance or a bounded
transformation law is demonstrated across the relevant readout maps, metrics,
radii, discretizations, numerical tolerances, backends, and equivalent runtime
realizations. Otherwise it must be described as an instrument-specific
measurement.

Bitwise reproducibility on one backend establishes reproducibility only for that
backend and instrument. It does not establish numerical equivalence on another
backend, quantization, kernel, topology, or serving path.

## Plane-selection and common-support gate

Plane selection is a feature-selection procedure and must occur entirely within
training or calibration populations.

Minimum plane families, where technically feasible, include:

1. active SAE-feature planes;
2. inactive or mixed-feature planes;
3. magnitude-, norm-, angle-, and condition-matched random planes;
4. task-scrambled and feature-label-scrambled planes;
5. PCA or other unsupervised raw-activation planes;
6. matched hidden-state-difference planes;
7. direct/logit-readout directions;
8. multiple SAE-family planes on the same base model and activation site;
9. functionally collapsed route or expert directions after separate admission;
10. random and null directions preserving the same selection budget.

Every comparison must report overlap of the matching variables and the exact
common-support population. Extrapolation outside common support is prohibited
for a confirmatory contrast. If a required comparison lacks support, its verdict
is `undefined`, not positive, negative, or inconclusive.

Plane-search count, rejected candidates, non-degeneracy filters, retries, seeds,
tie handling, and selection objectives are part of the experimental budget and
must be reported. Selecting the strongest plane after observing geometric or
outcome values is leakage unless confined to training and corrected by an
independent certification population.

## Center-construction and off-manifold gate

A natural activation and a constructed loop center are different scientific
objects.

Every study must report:

- distance from the natural activation to the loop center in Euclidean and
  instrument-relevant units;
- displacement relative to the loop radius;
- SAE reconstruction error at natural activations, centers, and every loop
  point;
- nearest-neighbor or other frozen activation-manifold proxies;
- whether matching rescales, projects, translates, interpolates, or otherwise
  changes the base point;
- sensitivity to alternative center constructions;
- results on naturally occurring matched activations or trajectory-derived
  perturbations where feasible;
- center and manifold diagnostics separately by plane type and outcome class.

A regression adjustment for one off-manifold proxy does not prove that the loop
is on-manifold. A high-dimensional covariance estimate that is rank deficient
cannot support an unqualified Mahalanobis claim. A nearest-neighbor proxy must
freeze the reference population, metric, dimension treatment, `k`, and task
leakage controls.

If a geometric effect disappears under natural-center or matched-displacement
controls, it is classified as center-construction or manifold-proximity evidence,
not semantic geometry.

## Transport-distortion gate

Restricted-Jacobian transport can accumulate rotation, stretch, shear,
conditioning error, pseudoinverse error, and discretization error.

Future studies must retain and report, at minimum:

- the complete accumulated transport operator before reducing it to one scalar;
- antisymmetric rotation and symmetric stretch/shear components separately;
- singular values, determinant, condition number, and distance from orthogonality;
- per-segment and accumulated pseudoinverse sensitivity;
- loop-closure and reverse-orientation checks;
- radius sweeps testing the expected small-loop area law;
- discretization sweeps and composition-order tests;
- determinant-floor and pseudoinverse-cutoff sensitivity;
- synthetic oracle cases with known rotation, shear, degeneracy, and zero
  curvature;
- cross-backend and cross-precision agreement after each runtime is separately
  admitted.

A scalar antisymmetric component does not establish that the measured operator
is a pure rotation. A holonomy difference correlated with shear, condition
number, closure error, center displacement, or numerical regularization remains
transport-distortion evidence until those alternatives are prospectively
resolved.

## SAE-family and training-recipe gate

An SAE feature is an artifact of a specific dictionary-learning procedure.
Feature identity and causal role may not be transferred across SAE families by
name, activation token, nearest-neighbor explanation, or decoder similarity.

Every SAE-based study must freeze:

- SAE family, source repository, immutable checkpoint, base model, activation
  site, tokenizer, corpus, training recipe, optimizer, seed, and revision;
- width, average and realized sparsity, TopK or threshold rule, activation
  function, encoder/decoder normalization, bias treatment, loss terms, and
  reconstruction metrics;
- feature-selection rule, activity threshold, explanation source, and handling
  of dead, duplicate, split, merged, and polysemantic features;
- dictionary alignment, matching uncertainty, and unmatched features when
  comparing families;
- train, calibration, certification, and sealed populations.

Where at least two independently trained SAE families exist for the same base
model and site, a causal or geometric claim must be replicated across families
or explicitly scoped to one family. Width sweeps within one recipe do not
substitute for cross-family replication.

Required controls include raw activation directions, PCA directions,
magnitude-matched decoder directions, shuffled-feature identities, decoder-norm
controls, random ablations, multiple activation functions where available, and
full-depth downstream recovery curves.

A locally redundant feature can still participate in a distributed code. A
large immediate logit effect can recover downstream. Neither local ablation nor
final-output recovery alone establishes global causal necessity or irrelevance.

## Claim boundary

The following claims remain separate:

1. the instrument is numerically valid;
2. a geometric response differs between frozen plane populations;
3. the response is stable across seeds and runtime realizations;
4. the response is stable across SAE families;
5. the response is associated with a semantic label;
6. the response identifies a unique mechanism;
7. the response causally affects computation;
8. the response predicts an independent objective outcome;
9. the response adds value beyond cheaper monitors;
10. a policy using the response improves utility under full cost and risk;
11. the policy is safe for production.

No earlier claim establishes a later one.

Terms such as `semantic curvature`, `meaning concentration`, `workspace
geometry`, `error curvature`, `mechanism plane`, or `intrinsic Jacobian geometry`
require direct prospective evidence for the named interpretation. Otherwise the
result must use bounded language such as `instrument-specific holonomy on the
selected plane population`.

## Mandatory cheaper comparators

Before a holonomy, curvature, SAE-plane, route-conditioned Jacobian, or other
geometric score can claim objective monitor value, it must demonstrate sealed
incremental value over compatible cheaper comparators at the same decision
boundary:

1. deterministic external checks and independent verifiers;
2. task, context, position, length, stage, cache, runtime, and serving metadata;
3. logits, entropy, margin, confidence, and explicit self-judgement;
4. raw hidden states and matched hidden-state differences;
5. direct and logit-lens readouts and their matched differences;
6. passive linear and nonlinear hidden-state probes;
7. bounded trajectory and program-state summaries;
8. random, mixed, PCA, and magnitude-matched plane statistics;
9. JVP norms, singular values, condition numbers, shear, stretch, closure error,
   and center-displacement diagnostics;
10. multiple SAE-family feature and ablation results where available;
11. dense-sibling and peer-representation baselines;
12. nominal and functional route telemetry after separate route admission;
13. sparse-feature or transcoder comparators after separate artifact admission.

A geometric score that is explained by conditioning, shear, plane magnitude,
center displacement, task phase, SAE recipe, or visible trajectory state is
classified as instrument, geometry, representation, or process telemetry rather
than privileged correctness awareness.

## Intervention boundary

Projection removal, feature ablation, Jacobian-transpose injection, activation
addition, transport-based steering, route forcing, expert substitution, retry,
truncation, and early exit are interventions.

Every intervention creates a new execution path requiring fresh artifact,
forward, derivative, cache, route, calibration, safety, privacy, equal-cost,
rollback, and production admission. Passive geometric association cannot
authorize intervention.

Intervention studies must report valid-to-invalid damage, incumbent
preservation, recoverability, full continuation divergence, objective outcome,
resource cost, latency tails, fallback frequency, and fail-closed behavior.

## Established by this correction

The following are now binding program facts:

- Jacobian-derived holonomy is readout-, instrument-, center-, plane-, radius-,
  backend-, and numerical-rule-dependent unless invariance is demonstrated.
- Active SAE-feature directions are not presumed to carry greater geometric
  structure than matched controls.
- Common-support failure produces an undefined comparison rather than licensed
  extrapolation.
- Rotation, shear, stretch, conditioning, closure error, and numerical
  regularization must be reported separately.
- Natural activations and constructed matched centers are separate populations.
- SAE-family and training-recipe sensitivity is mandatory for SAE-based causal
  and geometric claims.
- Semantic association, geometric concentration, causal necessity, objective
  monitor value, intervention utility, and production safety remain separate.
- No privacy, sealed-data, verifier, provenance, derivative, intervention, or
  production gate is weakened.
- Q35Q remains blocked.
